# HMNTY Creative Network

The HMNTY Creative Network connects San Diego organizations with local creative talent through work-first discovery, accountable curation, and warm human introductions.

## Language

**Creative Network**:
The trusted local network through which HMNTY helps paid creative work find relevant people.
_Avoid_: Marketplace, job board, talent platform

**Creative**:
A San Diego–area person with viewable creative work and current availability, regardless of school, seniority, or professional network.
_Avoid_: Candidate, applicant, user

**Employer**:
An organization or representative bringing a credible opportunity for paid creative work.
_Avoid_: Buyer, client account

**Brief**:
A real employer's description of paid creative work, including enough context for HMNTY to identify relevant creatives.
_Avoid_: Job posting, prompt

**Wall**:
The work-first discovery surface where portfolio work appears before identity or credentials.
_Avoid_: Search results, directory

**Curator**:
A named HMNTY person accountable for approving a shortlist and deciding whether to make an introduction.
_Avoid_: Admin, algorithm

**Shortlist**:
A small, HMNTY-approved set of creatives relevant to one real brief.
_Avoid_: AI ranking, search results, matches

**Introduction**:
A warm, HMNTY-mediated connection between an employer and a creative for a specific brief.
_Avoid_: Match, application, notification

**Pilot success**:
An employer submits a paid-work brief and accepts an introduction to at least one real creative.
_Avoid_: Signup count, page views, suggested match
